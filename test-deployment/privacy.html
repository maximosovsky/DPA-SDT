Privacy Notice

This notice describes the synthetic-only testnet prototype operated by Maxim Osovsky. It does not claim that all wallet addresses are anonymous or that hosting infrastructure processes no data.

Site and local files

Vercel and GitHub Pages each serve a published version of this site and may process IP address, request metadata and access logs to deliver and secure it.

The browser processes the exact synthetic identity and bank trace fixture locally to calculate an SDT v2 SHA-256 commitment to all 13 fields. Historical v1 four-field hashes and public records are unchanged. No bank, identity or payment is independently verified. Do not enter real identity, passport or bank information: the unsalted public hash may be guessed and is not encryption. Local export downloads all plaintext fields, the hash schema and digest to your device for reproducible comparison; it does not clear the open form. You control any copies you retain or share; loss of a file cannot be reversed from its hash. The site does not host the full off-chain DPA text or evidence package.

Wallet and public chain

When you connect an injected wallet, the wallet and its RPC provider may process your wallet address, network, request metadata, contract reads and proposed or submitted transactions. The configured public RPC may be used to read formation records, including on an invitation without wallet connection.

Contract transactions can publish wallet addresses, hashes, agreement references, shares, signatures and test ETH activity to the public blockchain. Wallet addresses can be linked to individuals; hashes and references are not confidentiality protections.

External evidence

If you choose an optional external HTTPS evidence URL and click Retrieve, your browser contacts that third-party host, which may receive your IP address and request metadata. The request omits browser credentials and referrer; the host's own practices still apply.

You may instead import a local file. An ipfs:// reference is not fetched automatically by this site. No analytics or non-essential cookies were observed in the audited initial page loads; this is not a promise about future changes or hosting-provider/RPC logs.

Recipients and retention

Possible recipients are Vercel or GitHub Pages for page delivery, your injected wallet and its RPC provider for wallet activity, public network participants for on-chain records, and the external evidence host you choose. Locally exported files remain wherever you or recipients store them.

Public blockchain records and their replicated copies cannot be erased by this website. Off-chain evidence availability is not guaranteed. Hosting and RPC providers may retain logs under their own policies; their exact retention periods and processing locations have not been established here. No fixed deletion period is promised.

Requests

For privacy, access, correction, deletion or objection requests where applicable, contact the operator through the confirmed contact routes. Explain the relevant page and, if relevant, a public transaction or wallet address; do not post private keys, identity documents or sensitive personal data in a public issue.

Requests concerning copies controlled by the operator can be reviewed, but a blockchain record or third-party copy cannot be erased by the operator. The applicable rights depend on your circumstances and law; this notice is not a claim of blanket GDPR compliance.
