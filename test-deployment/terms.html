Separate agreement and evidence

The DPA formation flow creates a separate participant agreement with its own version, off-chain text, evidence and individual acceptance. These website Terms are not that DPA and do not accept it for you.

The participant is responsible for obtaining and reviewing the exact off-chain evidence before signing. A new SDT v2 hash commits to all 13 exact fictional identity and bank-trace inputs, without verifying a person, bank or payment. Historical hashes commit only to four fields; do not reinterpret old records as thirteen-field declarations. The local export holds plaintext inputs and a versioned reproducible SHA-256 schema, so protect the file. A public unsalted hash is guessable, not encryption; it cannot restore missing evidence, establish truth, guarantee availability or make rights legally enforceable.
