3 · Pledge or offer a loan
Percentage is of your CURRENT unpledged balance, not the full agreement. Collateral rounds down to whole units. Approval permits the verified loan contract to move your units; revoke in your wallet or via ERC1155 if no longer needed.
4 · Position lifecycle
Funding transfers exact test ETH into borrower credit. Borrower withdraws principal, repays exact principal plus rounded-up interest before deadline, then recovers collateral; lender withdraws repayment credit. Unfunded offers can be cancelled. After deadline, lender may claim default collateral.
