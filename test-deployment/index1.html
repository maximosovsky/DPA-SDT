Research & testing · public record and protocol

Identity basis, agreement and participation

A reusable Self-Declared Token (SDT) links a wallet to self-declared passport details; it is not a verified identity or unique-person certificate. A Decentralized Partnership Agreement (DPA) records a version and hash of separate off-chain terms, named participant wallets and shares, and their individual acceptances. The separate pre-authenticated route described in the research is not implemented.

Preserve a basis for later attribution

A signature alone does not identify a person. A participant has a reason to retain evidence connecting a wallet, declaration and assent if rights are disputed. A self-declared banking transaction identifier may support later documentary tracing; no bank issues or verifies the SDT. Neither attribution nor enforceable rights are guaranteed.

  3. 3 · AcceptEach listed wallet reviews exact evidence and individually signs; the hash does not preserve lost text.
  4. 4 · UnitsAfter all accept, claim allocated units. Sale and collateral/loan are optional experiments, not transfers of legal rights.

SDT declaration hashing

New SDT v2 hashes bind all 13 exact entered synthetic fields: name, birth date, passport number, transaction number, bank name, SWIFT/BIC, account number, transaction date, time, time zone, amount, currency and recipient. These fields are not independently verified and do not establish that a bank or transfer exists.
