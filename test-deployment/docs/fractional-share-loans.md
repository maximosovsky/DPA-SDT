# Fractional DPA units and bilateral test-ETH loans

Local experimental prototype, not a financial product, valuation, oracle, liquidation engine, legally effective assignment, or securities exemption. No external lender, automated underwriting, guaranteed loan, or faucet payout. The lender must voluntarily send the agreed native ETH on the selected test network. Do not use real assets or deploy without separate audit, provenance checks, and authorization.

## Model

`DPAShareUnits(formation)` creates one ERC-1155 collection scoped to one DPAFormation address/version. `UNIT_ID=1`; 10,000 units represent the whole agreement, with original allocation percentages multiplied by 100. Claim opens only after *all* listed participants have accepted and is available once to each listed accepting wallet. `claimed` is permanent even if the original holder transfers everything. `totalSupply <= 10000`; no public burn or additional mint path. ERC-1155 `balanceOf` reflects **current transferable holdings**; `formation.acceptances` reflects original signatures and does not change with transfers. The collection URI contains fixed on-chain JSON/SVG and immutable formation/chain/hash provenance, not personal text or a separate NFT for every fragment. An ERC-1155 transfer does **not** prove contractual rights have transferred; off-chain DPA terms and applicable law remain separate. SDT is untouched and remains identity-bound.

`DPAShareLoan(units)` provides two mutually separate escrow modes:

- **Standalone pledge:** borrower sets a designated counterparty, percentage of their **current spendable own balance**, and lifetime (1 second to 365 days). `createPledge` locks the computed ERC-1155 units. Counterparty alone can `releasePledge` to borrower early; borrower alone can `reclaimPledge` strictly after expiry. No ETH is lent in this mode, and the pledge does not let the counterparty seize units.
- **Bilateral funded loan:** borrower calls `createLoan(lender, percentBps, principalWei, interestBps, durationSeconds)` and escrows units. Named lender alone calls payable `fund(id)` with **exactly** `principalWei`; nobody auto-accepts or auto-funds. Immutable terms and deadline (funding block timestamp plus duration) are on-chain. Borrower withdraws principal via `withdraw()`; borrower calls payable `repay(id)` with exact `repaymentAmount(id)` up to and including deadline. Lender withdraws repayment via `withdraw()`. Borrower calls `claimRepaidCollateral(id)`. Before funding, borrower can `cancelLoan(id)` and recover units. Strictly after deadline while unpaid, only named lender can `claimDefault(id)` and receive the escrowed units, with no oracle or partial liquidation. Only one terminal transition can happen. Credits are pull-payments and may be withdrawn after collateral settlement. Unexpected direct ERC-1155 transfers and batch transfers to the escrow revert.

**Precision:** Percentages use basis points (`10000=100%`). Collateral is `floor(currentBalance * percentBps / 10000)`, with a zero result rejected. `quoteCollateral(borrower,percentBps)` returns `(unitAmount,remainderNumerator)`; UI must show remainder/rounding, rather than implying an exact percentage. E.g. a holder of 4000 units (40% whole DPA) pledging 2500 bps (25% of their own share) locks 1000 units (10% of whole DPA), **not** 25% of the whole agreement. Interest is `ceil(principalWei * interestBps / 10000)` wei for the full term, not annualized; no accrual or valuation. Loan principal and repayment are native ETH only; on Base Sepolia these are test ETH, not faucet entitlement or money automatically issued by the app.

## UI integration contract

Deploy `DPAFormation`, then `DPAShareUnits(formationAddress)`, then `DPAShareLoan(unitsAddress)` on the **same verified chain**. `src/share-units.ts` exports `unitsAbi`, `loanAbi`, `quoteOwnShare`, `quoteRepayment`, and `readPosition`. `readPosition(client,loanAddress,id,chainId,expectedUnitsAddress)` validates chain, code presence and unit binding, but is **not canonical runtime bytecode verification**; use deployment manifest/runtime provenance checks before permitting signing in a public interface. Never infer public addresses from local tests. Verify wallet account anew before every transaction.

Show own `balanceOf(wallet,1)`, `quoteCollateral(wallet,bps)`, `isApprovedForAll(wallet,loan)`, `positions(id)`, `credits(wallet)` and `repaymentAmount(id)` before enabling action. `setApprovalForAll(loan,true)` gives the escrow permission over **all** current/future ERC-1155 units in that collection: show the spender and this broad scope prominently and provide revoke controls. A percent refers to wallet balance **at transaction execution**, not historical allocation; re-quote immediately before signing. Use explicit separate actions for pledge, offer, lender funding, borrower credit withdrawal, repayment, collateral claim, default, cancellation, and lender credit withdrawal. Display immutable terms, signer, status, chain, collateral units/whole-DPA percentage, precise rounding, repayment amount and deadline. Never label an offer as a funded loan.

`positions(id)` fields: `(borrower,counterparty,collateral,percentBps,principal,interestBps,duration,deadline,status)`; enum `None=0,Pledge=1,Offered=2,Funded=3,Repaid=4,Closed=5,Defaulted=6`. `nextId` starts at 1. Methods/events: units `claim`, `claimed`, `totalSupply`, `balanceOf`, `safeTransferFrom`, `uri`; loan `createPledge`, `createLoan`, `quoteCollateral`, `fund`, `repaymentAmount`, `repay`, `withdraw`, `cancelLoan`, `releasePledge`, `reclaimPledge`, `claimRepaidCollateral`, `claimDefault`; events `Opened`, `Funded`, `Repaid`, `Closed`. Solidity events and returned IDs should be resolved from receipts rather than assuming every transaction's ID from a stale read.

## Verification boundary

Run `npx hardhat test test/DPAShareUnits.test.ts test/DPAShareLoan.test.ts` for isolated simulated-EVM multi-wallet lifecycle tests; local accounts and synthetic identities only. This does not establish Base Sepolia deployment, safe valuations, or external-contract adversarial audit. No public financial transaction was submitted.
